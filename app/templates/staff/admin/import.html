Select all the files at once (hold Ctrl and click each one): patients, progress_notes, treatment_plans, bills, notes, prescriptions and certificates. export_errors is ignored. Or upload the export .zip as it is. Nothing is saved until you review the preview and confirm.
The export doesn't say which branch each patient belongs to. All branches: staff of every branch can open these patients, like in MyMedsPH, and their recall follow-ups show in every branch. Dentists still see only the patients assigned to them. Patients who were already imported keep their setting.

How updating works

  • To bring in the latest from MyMedsPH: 1. upload the newest export here; 2. then upload the newest collection report on MyMedsPH bills → real bills. Always in that order, so each payment finds its bill. Old files can be used again: anything already imported is skipped.
  • Safe to repeat. Patients are matched by their MyMedsPH patient ID. Records already imported are skipped, also when a bill's status (e.g. now paid) or a note's remarks changed in MyMedsPH since. Changes made in MyMedsPH to records already here aren't copied over: make those here.
  • Contact details are refreshed from the file (name, mobile, email, address), but a blank value never erases what's already here.
  • Health history is only filled when it's empty, so edits your dentists made here are kept.
  • Old bills come in as history on each patient's record. To have them as real bills (balances, sales, commission), use Turn MyMedsPH bills into real bills.
  • Privacy consent isn't in the MyMedsPH export, so imported patients show "Needs consent" and get no reminders until you record their consent.
  • The uploaded file is deleted after the import. It isn't stored on the server.
