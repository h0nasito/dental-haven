3. Convert

  • Lines of the same patient on the same day become one bill, numbered MM-…, dated that day.
  • Paid: the bill gets a payment of its full amount on that day, shown as "Paid in MyMedsPH".
  • Unpaid: the bill keeps its balance, to collect like any bill.
  • The dentist is left empty because MyMedsPH doesn't export it. Set it on a bill line when needed, and commission follows.
  • These bills count in Sales & collections and reports for their dates. Cash counts aren't affected, because "Paid in MyMedsPH" isn't cash.
