What happens when you confirm

  • Every MyMedsPH bill line becomes a real bill (one per patient per day), unless its status is set to "Keep as history".
  • Each payment goes to the patient's bills: first a bill with the procedure it paid for, else the oldest bill with a balance. A payment bigger than a bill's balance is split over the next bills.
  • If a patient paid more than their MyMedsPH bills add up to, the extra becomes account credit.
  • The payment keeps its date, branch, payment type (cash, GCash, bank…) and remarks. The dentist from the report is set on the bill line for that procedure, when the dentist has an account here.
  • Payments count in Sales & collections for their dates. Uploading the same reports again adds nothing twice.
