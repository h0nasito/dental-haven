Good habits

  • Download a backup every week, and before big changes such as an import from MyMedsPH.
  • Keep at least two copies in different places (e.g. an encrypted USB drive and a private cloud folder).
  • The server also saves the database by itself every night at 11:30 PM and keeps 14 days (DigitalOcean setup).
  • Not included: the server settings file (/etc/dental-haven/env, with the secret key and email passwords). Keep a copy of it separately.
