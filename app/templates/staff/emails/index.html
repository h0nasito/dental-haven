Who receives what

  • Promotions: only patients who said yes to promotions (“OK to receive promotions/newsletters” on their profile).
  • Announcements (e.g. holiday schedule, new branch): patients with privacy consent who haven't opted out.
  • Birthday greetings: the same as announcements; the birthday promo only for those who accept promotions.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. Patients can stop promotions only, or all clinic emails. It takes effect right away, even for emails still waiting to send.
  • Deleted patients and patients without an email are skipped.
