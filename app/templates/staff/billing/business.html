Where the numbers come from
  • Gross collections: money received this month (payments and deposits, less refunds), as in the Financial report.
  • Expenses: entries in Expenses and Monthly bills by the date paid (posted and not yet posted), plus lab fees on bills paid. Categories are grouped: supplies & lab = Dental supplies, Laboratory fees, lab fees on bills; fixed overhead = rent, utilities, internet, equipment, repairs, permits; administrative & marketing = marketing, professional fees, office, transport, pantry, food, miscellaneous.
  • Payroll: “Salaries & wages” expenses, dentist commissions, and approved payroll runs ending this month (gross pay).
  • A month that isn't over is compared with the same days of the month before.
